Our Story

Who We Are

Welcome to Greenline Farmers, your trusted source for delicious, hand-picked strawberries and goldenberries. We are passionate about delivering fresh, healthy, and high-quality fruits to our customers with love and care.

Founded in 1995, we have spent three decades perfecting our growing methods — combining modern sustainable farming with traditional care to produce berries of exceptional quality.

Our Mission

To provide farm-fresh berries while maintaining the highest standards of quality, hygiene, and customer satisfaction — while preserving the environment for future generations.

Why Choose Us

  • Fresh and organic strawberries & goldenberries
  • Zero chemicals — grown with nature
  • Hand-picked at peak ripeness daily
  • Direct farm-to-table delivery
  • Customer-first service since 1995

"Fresh from nature, grown with love."

Every berry we grow is a promise — to our customers, to the land, and to the future of sustainable farming.
