How We Grow

Goldenberries — known as Physalis peruviana — thrive in our cool, misty highland climate. We prepare well-drained, slightly acidic soil enriched with natural compost, creating the ideal foundation for healthy, vigorous plants.

Planted from cuttings and grown under minimal intervention, each berry develops slowly inside its papery husk — nature's own packaging. Drip irrigation and hand-weeding ensure every plant receives the care it deserves.

At peak ripeness, when the berries turn a deep amber-gold, our farmers carefully hand-harvest each fruit, preserving the husk for natural freshness. From field to your hands — pure, honest, extraordinary.
