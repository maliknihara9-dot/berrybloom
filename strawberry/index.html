From Soil to Table

How We Grow

Strawberries begin their journey in carefully prepared, well-drained sandy loam soil enriched with organic compost. Raised beds improve drainage and airflow, while drip irrigation delivers water directly to the roots — minimising waste and disease.

As plants flower and fruit develops, straw mulch retains moisture and keeps berries clean. Each strawberry is hand-picked at peak ripeness, ensuring only the sweetest, healthiest fruits travel from our fields to your table.
